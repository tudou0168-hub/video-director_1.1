# 04 Typography Extraction Rules

## Hierarchy

| Level | Use | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Hook, result, painful truth | 88-140px |
| Section | Top label, status, template name | 18-28px |
| Card Title | Short component title | 34-56px |
| Card Body | 1-3 short bullets | 24-34px |
| Subtitle | Chinese spoken captions | 46-56px |

## Chinese First

All viewer-facing subtitles are Chinese. English can appear only as product/tool names or short label fragments such as `API`, `HUD`, `Agent`, `Codex`, `Hermes`.

## Density Rule

If the frame needs more than 18 Chinese characters to explain the main idea, rewrite the visual. Do not shrink the font to fit a weak sentence.
